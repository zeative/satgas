Yours to run

Satgas is open source under AGPL-3.0. It runs on your laptop or your server, with your own model sign-in or keys.

  • Your folder. Boards, files and settings stay on the machine you run it on.
  • Your keys. Agents reach outside accounts only through the connectors you add.
  • Needs Bun 1.2 or newer, and Claude Code or the Codex CLI.

Questions

What does the name mean

Satgas is Indonesian for satuan tugas, a task force: a team put together for one job.

Does my code leave my machine

Your files stay in your folder. The agents send their prompts to the model provider you pick, exactly as Claude Code or Codex would in your terminal.

Which models can it use

Claude Code serves your Claude sign-in or subscription, the Anthropic API, DeepSeek, Qwen, Z.ai, OpenRouter and Ollama. Codex serves your Codex sign-in, the OpenAI API, Groq and LM Studio. Pick one model and the whole team runs on it.

What does it cost

Satgas is free. You pay your model provider what you would pay to use Claude Code or Codex yourself, and Satgas shows the tokens and the estimated cost.

Is it ready for real work

It is early, at version 0.1. Expect rough edges and breaking changes, and please open an issue when you hit one.
